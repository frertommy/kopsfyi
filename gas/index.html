How it's constructed

Rocket and feathers.

01

Asymmetric pass-through

Retail rises faster than it falls. The fit captures separate up/down cumulative pass-through from wholesale — the classic "rockets and feathers" asymmetry — on 16y of EIA weekly data.

02

Wholesale leads

NY-Harbor gasoline spot and WTI move first; the pump follows over ~weeks. The pipeline gap measures how much of the latest wholesale move hasn't reached the pump yet.

03

Error-correction + ratchet

An error-correction term pulls retail back to its long-run wholesale relationship; arch_b ratchets to the weekly EIA print. 1-week forecast RMSE ≈ –.
