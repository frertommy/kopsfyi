How it's constructed

Claims lead the print.

01

Jobless claims signal

Initial and continuing claims (ICSA/CCSA) print weekly, well before payrolls. Their month-over-month change is the leading input to the nowcast.

02

Payroll trend

The recent NFP trajectory anchors the level; the claims signal nudges it. Fit ex-COVID — claims-only means an honestly wide band.

03

First-print ratchet

Settles to the BLS first-print — what the market reacts to. Monthly + annual CES benchmark revisions enter only as low-weight observations.
