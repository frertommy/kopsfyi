What the cycle rewards

Three lenses on the same flow.

Where to look as the money moves — each lens is becoming a KOPS oracle.

01

Follow the checks

Hyperscaler capex — and the backlog (RPO) behind it — funds the entire build-out. That spend, and the ratio of receivers to writers, is the single largest forward signal. Tracked by Capex & Check-Flow.

02

Value capture

As money flows down the chain, margin pools wherever supply is scarce — accelerators and HBM keep the largest slice, commodity layers the least. Tracked by Value-Capture.

03

The physical floor

Electricity, transformers and cooling now gate how much compute can run at all — the bottleneck has moved from silicon to power. Tracked by AI-Power.
