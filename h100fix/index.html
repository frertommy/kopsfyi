Index methodology

One engine, two quotes.

01

The level

H100FIX is the weighted geometric mean of the three tier medians, in natural units: $/GPU-hr. Same legs, same frozen 40/35/25 weights, same ≤14-day carry governance as KH100 — computed from the same tick at the same instant, so the fix and the index can never disagree.

02

The identity

FIX = KH100 ÷ 100 × $3.0279 — the base-day fix (2026-07-03). One methodology, two projections: quote KH100 to talk about change, quote H100FIX to talk about money.

03

Honest dollars

Dollar levels are never spliced or rebased. Pre-July-2026 history is the neocloud tier's real dollars alone, drawn as its own labeled line. Ornn's transacted print rides beside the fix as the market-truth cross-check — never averaged in.
