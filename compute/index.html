How it's built

Provider-median, every tier.

01

Across providers

For each GPU we take the median posted rate across every tracked cloud — not one provider's sticker. A representative price of the market, not a marketing number.

02

On-demand + spot

We publish both the guaranteed on-demand rate and the preemptible spot rate. The spot discount is a live scarcity gauge — it widens in a glut, compresses in a squeeze.

03

Honest scope

These are provider-median list/spot rates, not a single transacted-price index — that one (Ornn/Silicon) is Bloomberg-gated. Free, transparent, weekly back to 2024.
