What does an H100-hour actually rent for?

There is no single H100 price. The same GPU-hour clears from ~$1.8 on marketplaces to $12+ at big clouds — a 2–7× spread that any one-source number hides. KH100 prints the stratified composite of the three tiers the market actually clears at, the dispersion between them, and the committed-tenor curve — daily, from a self-archived book of ~600 offers, with Ornn's transacted index printed beside as the cross-check.

The problem
Every published "H100 price" is one seller tier. Big clouds quote $4–12, neoclouds ~$3, marketplaces clear under $2.6 — same silicon. A single number isn't a price, it's a sampling choice.

Index methodology

Stratified, frozen, cross-checked.

01

The tiers

A — big-cloud on-demand median via gpuhunt (AWS/GCP/OCI/Lambda + 4 more, ~600 H100 offers/day). B — GetDeploying neocloud list median (~40 providers). C — marketplace clearing: geomean of Vast.ai asks (full explicit-query pool, verification-tiered) and Akash on-chain leases. The full per-offer book is archived daily — the index is reconstructable from raw offers.

02

The composite

Fixed-weight geometric mean of tier relatives, base day 2026-07-03 = 100 ($4.19 / $2.8725 / $1.9384 — frozen v0, never restated; changes version). Pre-period context is a tier-B-only weekly spine back to Jan 2025, rebased to join at 100 and labeled as such.

03

Governance

Per-leg carry-last-good ≤ 14 days, else the print suspends. Ornn transacted and CCIR reference rates are cross-checks, never inputs. Measurement upgrades (Vast full-pool query, 2026-08-30) are documented splices, not silent restatements. Full spec in the KOPS methodology annex.
