How it works

Many markets, one signal.

01

Each market is a noisy read

The stock market, the dollar and bond yields each say something about how easy money is — and each is noisy on its own. Stocks up = looser; a strong dollar and high yields = tighter.

02

Standardize & pool

We score each market against its own recent history — how far above or below normal it sits — then pool those scores into one number. Pooling cancels the noise any single market carries.

03

One dial, all Kalshi

The result is a 0–100 dial: 50 is neutral, above 50 is tight, below 50 is loose. Built entirely from live prediction markets — no surveys, no lagged official data.

FinX is built from the highest-volume markets on Kalshi — the S&P 500 and Nasdaq alone carry millions in open interest. It's the dial the Fed steers by, with no free public equivalent.
