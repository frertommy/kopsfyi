1 · Robot labor cost

When does a robot beat a wage?

The cost of an hour of robot labor vs an hour of human labor (US manufacturing production wage, live from FRED). Two answers: amortized robot hardware is already a rounding error next to a wage — and commercial robot-labor-as-a-service has now reached parity too.
