How it's constructed

Treasury receipts, not a survey.

01

Daily customs feed

The Daily Treasury Statement reports customs duties deposited every business day — month-to-date and fiscal-year-to-date. This is the actual money collected, days ahead of any monthly report.

02

Effective-rate denominator

Annualized customs duties ÷ annualized goods imports (Census/FRED BOPGIMP) = the realized effective tariff rate the US is actually charging.

03

Crowd overlay

The Kalshi KXEFFTARIFF and KXTARIFFRATEPRC markets price the effective rate and the China rate. The gap to the receipts data is the signal — there's no derivatives market to copy.
