The basis

Prediction market vs the derivatives.

Two markets price the same thing — where the Fed funds rate lands by year-end. The prediction market (Kalshi KXRATECUTCOUNT) and the derivatives market (CME Fed Funds futures) rarely agree. The gap between them is the basis — and when one is right, it's a signal in the other.
